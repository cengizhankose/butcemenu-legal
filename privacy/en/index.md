# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11.11.2026

This policy explains which data the Bütçe Menü iOS app ("the app") processes and why. Data controller: Cengizhan Köse. Contact: [kose651@gmail.com](mailto:kose651@gmail.com)

## In short

- No account. We do not ask for your name, e-mail or phone number.
- No location permission. You pick your city and district from a list.
- No ad tracking: we do not access the advertising identifier (IDFA) and do not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission. There are no ads in the app.
- We do not sell your data.

## Data we process

| Data | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Anonymous install id** (a random id created on each install) | To link plan sync, analytics and subscription status to the same install | On your device; at the service providers below |
| **Plan data**: the nights you cook, household size, chosen supermarkets, weekly budget, chosen city/district, the generated menu and shopping list | To build and store your plan and keep it from being lost | On your device; on Convex servers, keyed by the anonymous install id |
| **Usage analytics**: events such as screens opened and buttons tapped; device model, iOS and app version | To understand how the app is used and improve it | PostHog (EU cloud) |
| **Purchase status**: subscription type, trial/active/expired state, transaction details | To unlock Premium and verify the subscription | Apple and Adapty |

Payments are taken by Apple. We never see your card details or your Apple ID.

The app does not ask for allergies, health data or any other special category of personal data.

## Service providers

- **Convex**: backend where plan data is stored anonymously.
- **PostHog** (EU cloud): usage analytics.
- **Adapty**: subscription and paywall management; verifies Apple purchase information.
- **Apple**: payment and subscriptions through the App Store.

These providers process data only on our behalf and for the purposes above. Their servers may be outside Türkiye (in the EU and other countries), so data may be transferred abroad.

## Legal basis

We process data to run the app and provide the service you purchased (performance of a contract) and on our legitimate interest in improving the app (Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, Art. 5/2-c and 5/2-f).

## Retention

Plan data and analytics events are kept while you use the app or until you ask us to delete them. Purchase records may be kept as long as required by law.

## Your rights and data deletion

Under Art. 11 of Law No. 6698 you may request access to, correction of and deletion of your data.

- Deleting the app removes plans and settings from your device.
- To delete the anonymous records on our servers, e-mail [kose651@gmail.com](mailto:kose651@gmail.com). As there is no account, we may ask you for details to find your record. We answer requests within 30 days.
- You can cancel the subscription in iPhone **Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions**. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes

If we update this policy, we change the date on this page.
