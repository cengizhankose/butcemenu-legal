# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13.11.2026

This policy explains what data the Kazan app (the App) collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and how you can delete it. The App is developed by Cengizhan Köse, an independent developer and private individual, who is responsible for your data. The Turkish version (Gizlilik Politikası) and the KVKK disclosure notice (KVKK Aydınlatma Metni) are available in the App; if the versions differ, the Turkish text prevails.

## In short

- No account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
- No location permission; you pick the city, district and store branch yourself.
- No sensitive data such as health or allergy information.
- Usage analytics are sent only if you opt in. The setting is off by default and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.
- We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, we do not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA) and we do not sell your data. The App shows no ads.
- Settings > Gizlilik > Verilerimi sil deletes all your data on our servers in one tap.

## 1. Data we collect and why

- Install ID: a random identifier created on your device when the App first opens. It is not linked to your name or contact details, but because it distinguishes your installation we treat it as personal data. Used to store your plans on our server, recognise your subscription and, if you opt in, group analytics events.
- Plan preferences: household size, the evenings you cook, weekly budget, selected stores (chain, branch, city and district; the branch's OpenStreetMap ID when available), ingredients you already have and your street-market preference, if provided. Used to build and price the menu.
- Plan and list data: the plan's week, dishes and swaps, selected store, totals per store, items ticked on the shopping list, shopping channel, created and updated times. Saved automatically so your plan is not lost and comes back if you reinstall the App.
- Usage analytics (only with consent): the names and properties of steps in the App (for example household size, budget and plan total, selected supermarket chain, paywall steps), install ID, app version, operating system, Premium status and, if you opened the App from a campaign link, the first campaign parameters (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, code). Used to see where people get stuck and improve the App.
- Subscription data (only if you purchase): install ID, Apple transaction IDs and receipt information, product, trial and subscription status, dates, price and currency. Used to unlock, verify and restore Premium.

Without consent, no analytics event is sent or queued on your device, and campaign parameters never leave your device.

## 2. Why analytics are off by default

Our analytics go to an external provider (PostHog) and carry a persistent install ID, so we rely on your explicit consent. That is why the setting is off when you first open the App, "İzin ver" (allow) and "Hayır" (no) are offered with equal weight, and you can withdraw consent at any time in Settings. This follows the criteria in the Turkish Data Protection Board's Cookie Guidelines.

## 3. Service providers we share data with

- Convex, Inc. (USA): database and server. Your plan data is stored in Convex's European Union region (Ireland).
- PostHog, Inc. (USA): product analytics, only with consent. We use PostHog's EU cloud (Frankfurt, Germany); raw client IP addresses are discarded from stored events.
- Adapty Tech Inc. (USA): subscription infrastructure, with servers in the USA. IP address and advertising identifier (IDFA) collection are disabled; forwarding subscription events to other analytics services is turned off.
- Apple: Apple takes the payment. We never see your card details.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us and on our instructions. Because their servers are outside Türkiye, your data is transferred to the European Union and the USA.

## 4. Retention

- Data on your device stays until you delete the App or tap Verilerimi sil.
- Plan and list data on our server is deleted automatically 12 months after it was last updated.
- Usage analytics are kept by PostHog for 1 year and deleted when that period ends.
- The subscription record is kept at Adapty for as long as the subscription needs to be verified. The purchase itself stays with Apple.

## 5. Deleting your data and withdrawing consent

- Settings > Gizlilik > Verilerimi sil deletes your plans, usage analytics and subscription record on our servers, resets the plan and preferences on your device and creates a new install ID. Your subscription continues at Apple; to cancel it, go to Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone. You can re-link an active subscription with "Satın alımları geri yükle" (Restore Purchases).
- Turn usage analytics off at any time in Settings > Gizlilik. New events stop and events waiting on your device are deleted.
- For any other request, email me@cengizhankose.com and include the support ID shown at the bottom of the Settings screen. We respond within 30 days, free of charge.

## 6. Security

Data is sent to our providers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Only the developer can access the data on the server. The server only lets a record be reached with that record's install ID.

## 7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe such data has reached us, email me@cengizhankose.com and we will delete it.

## 8. Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we publish the new version in the App and at the link on the App Store page, with the date at the top updated. If we plan to use your data for a new purpose, we will tell you before we start. Questions: Cengizhan Köse, me@cengizhankose.com.
